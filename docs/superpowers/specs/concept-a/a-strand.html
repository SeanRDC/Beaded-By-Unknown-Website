Before you order

How long does it take?

Everything is a pre-order. Allow 7 to 14 days for your piece to be made, checked and packed before it ships by J&T Express.

What does shipping cost?

₱85 to Metro Manila, ₱100 to Luzon, ₱120 to Visayas and ₱130 to Mindanao. Free on orders over ₱500.

How do I measure my wrist?

Wrap a soft tape around your wrist, snug but not tight, and read it in centimetres. The builder works out the bead count for you.

Can I return a piece?

Each piece is made for you, so all sales are final once work has started. Check your measurement before you order.

How can I pay?

GCash, Maya, QR Ph or card, through PayMongo.
